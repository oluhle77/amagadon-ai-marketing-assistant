# Amagadon Approved Social Post Examples

## Purpose

These examples demonstrate the quality, tone, structure and platform
adaptation expected from Amagadon.

They are prototype reference examples created specifically for the
Amagadon AI Fluency Project.

They are NOT evidence of:

- real company activities
- customer results
- product performance
- organisational achievements
- research findings

Amagadon should learn the communication style and structure from these
examples without treating example content as factual organisational
information.


# LinkedIn Examples

## Example 1: Authority and Insight

### Topic

Practical AI fluency in the workplace

### Post

Knowing what AI is and knowing how to use it effectively at work are two
different things.

For organisations, the opportunity is not simply giving employees access
to AI tools. It is helping people build the practical capability and
judgement to apply those tools to appropriate workplace challenges.

That can involve identifying a real task, giving AI useful context,
reviewing the result and refining the approach.

The technology matters. The ability to use it responsibly matters as
well.

How is your organisation helping employees move from AI awareness to
practical capability?

#AIFluency #FutureOfWork #WorkplaceLearning

### Why This Example Works

- Opens with a professional distinction.
- Provides useful business context.
- Focuses on practical application.
- Keeps human judgement visible.
- Avoids unsupported statistics.
- Ends with a relevant engagement CTA.
- Uses a limited number of relevant hashtags.


## Example 2: Human and Conversational

### Topic

Learning to use AI at work

### Post

What is one task in your working week that you would like to explore with
AI?

Start there.

Give the AI a clear task.
Provide useful context.
Review what comes back.
Refine the instructions if needed.
Decide whether the result is suitable to use.

Practical AI fluency is not only about knowing what an AI tool can do. It
also involves learning how to use it appropriately in real work.

Which task would you experiment with first?

#AIFluency #WorkplaceLearning

### Why This Example Works

- Starts with a question rather than an unsupported generalisation.
- Uses short lines for readability.
- Connects AI to everyday work.
- Encourages practical experimentation.
- Keeps human review visible.
- Ends with a relevant engagement question.
